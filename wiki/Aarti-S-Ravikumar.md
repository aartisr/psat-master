## 🕊️ Why I Built This Sanctuary

I look at modern educational technology and I feel a profound sense of urgency. We live in an era where software often treats users as metrics, optimizing for "session time" and click-through rates rather than true comprehension. In test preparation, this has translated into aggressive paywalls, heavy data scraping, and invasive sign-up flow-walls.

I asked myself a simple question: *What if we built a digital study room that treated the human mind with the dignity it deserves?*

**PSAT Master** is my answer. I built this platform because I believe that high-quality, high-utility test preparation should be a public good, as free and accessible as a public library. Inside this environment, you will find:
1. **Uninterrupted Concentration**: No popups, no tracking cookies, and no payment prompts.
2. **Deep Diagnostic Support**: A custom-designed **Mistake Notebook** that treats errors not as failures, but as precious nodes of growth.
3. **Adaptive Velocity**: Smart drills that meet you exactly at your baseline and guide you upward at your own natural pace.
