## 🕊️ A Vision of Zero-Friction Education

Standardized exams like the Digital PSAT/NMSQT & SAT are more than just gates; they are defining thresholds in a young student's life. But preparation has become cluttered with subscription gates, noisy telemetry trackers, and high price tags that divide those who can afford guidance from those who cannot. 

I designed **PSAT Master** as a personal, heartfelt promise. It is an act of pure reverence for your concentration. Inside this ecosystem, there are:
* **No Sign-Up Screens**: You do not need to give away your email, your name, or your personal info to study.
* **No Advertisements or Up-Sells**: There is zero cognitive noise to pull your focus away from the page.
* **Pure Local Privacy**: Your stats, completed exams, and mistake notebooks belong completely to you—persisted securely in your browser's local sandbox.
