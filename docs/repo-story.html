The Genesis Narrative

From a personal promise to a public good.

Standardized prep is an industry. But learning is a relationship. Read the story of why we created PSAT Master and why we are fighting to keep test prep pure, private, and open to all.

reclaiming focus in a noisy world

Standardized exams like the Digital PSAT and SAT represent a critical threshold. In a healthy educational ecosystem, preparing for these checkpoints would be a period of self-discovery—where young minds expand their reasoning, challenge themselves, and build intellectual confidence.

But the software built to help students cross this threshold often feels more like a shopping mall than a library. Users are met with intrusive sign-up popups, premium tiers that hide high-yield questions, and tracker networks that capitalize on private data.

I built PSAT Master to carve out a quiet space for study. By stripping away authentication walls, tracking scripts, and subscription requirements, we've created a zero-friction playground designed to respect your focus and save your precious energy.
